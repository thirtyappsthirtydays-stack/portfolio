What the rule does

Three items count against capital only above a threshold: deferred tax assets from temporary differences, mortgage servicing assets, and significant investments in the common stock of unconsolidated financial institutions.

Each item is deducted where it exceeds 10% of the CET1 base. Whatever remains of the three combined is deducted where it exceeds 15% of CET1 after all deductions.

Logic

v3.2
# CET1 base after rules R-CET1-01 to -13
base = cet1_elements − prior_deductions
limit_10 = 0.10 × base
for item in (dta_temp, msa_net, sig_inv):
over_10[item] = max(0, item − limit_10)
remaining = Σ (item − over_10[item])
# equals 15% of CET1 after all deductions
limit_15 = 0.1765 × (base − Σ item)
over_15 = max(0, remaining − limit_15)
deduction = Σ over_10 + over_15
