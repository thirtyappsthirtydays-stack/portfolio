03 · Develop

Three decisions shaped the flow

Several shipped as working versions first, and the client’s feedback decided which stayed.

How should the form ask? For a relative answering about someone else.

  • CutA · Long formNine fields on one page. It read like a hospital form.
  • ShippedB · One question per screenSix questions, with a counter and Enter to continue
  • Side doorC · WhatsApp onlyKept as a pre-filled message, on every screen

How do records arrive? They need to reach him before the call.

  • CutA · Upload in the formCT and PET-CT files are too large for a web form
  • CutB · E-Nabız loginA portal password stored in a sheet
  • ShippedC · WhatsApp, after bookingA list of what to send, at least 24 hours ahead

How does money move? Upfront, into a Turkish account.

  • Not builtA · Card checkoutFees and set-up before the first booking
  • ShippedB · Bank transfer, then confirmFee and IBAN on one screen, then a “sent it?” check
Why pay before picking a timeEvery slot in the surgeon’s week belongs to someone who has already paid.
Layouts that hold Turkish and EnglishNeither language is always longerSo buttons size to their label and headings wrap.Capitals need the right languageTurkish has a dotted İ and a dotless ı, so the language switch also sets lang.
