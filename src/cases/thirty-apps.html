Personal challenge · Web and mobile · March 2026

30 Apps in 30 Days

Learning AI-assisted product development, one app a day

To see how AI is changing the way products get built, I set myself a challenge: 30 small web and mobile apps in 30 days, each one built with AI coding tools to solve a real problem.

How I worked

Start from a problem, then write it down

Every app began with a real frustration. Before any prompting, I wrote a short product requirements document: the problem, the user flow and the core features.

One app, one day
Notice a problemWrite a short PRDBuild with an AI toolRevise the PRD and iterateA working prototype

I often drafted the PRD with AI, then edited it, and revised it several times as each app took shape.
