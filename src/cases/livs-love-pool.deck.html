Results

More personal than swiping

In usability testing, people responded well to the guided sign-up and the personal tone.

For singles90%Of singles who started signing up finished their profile
For Olivia40→10 hOlivia’s admin time each month
What testers said
  • The profile prompts felt more meaningful than dating-app profiles
  • How It Works built trust and made the process clear
  • The date feedback loop felt engaging, and kept people accountable
  • The whole experience felt more personal than swipe-based apps
What I learnedDesign for both sidesDesigning for Catherine and Olivia together improved the experience for both.
