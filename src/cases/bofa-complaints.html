My Design Approach: The UX Double Diamond

The first diamond settled what each team needed from the data. The second settled how each view would show it.

What each team asked of the data

The same complaints, three different questions.

Compliance

Are complaints resolved on time and handled within policy?

Deadlines, the age of open cases, and the rule each issue falls under.

Risk

Where is customer harm building, and how fast?

Trends, severity, and new themes in what customers write.

Legal

Which complaints carry legal or regulatory exposure?

Single cases, the customer’s exact words, and patterns across similar complaints.

02 · Define

One source, read three ways

The three teams needed the same numbers, arranged around their own questions.

Problem statement

Compliance, Risk and Legal relied on complaint data every day. Finding the figures they needed took too long, and the reports were not shaped around how each team worked.

How might we

How might we give each team a view built around its own questions, with every view drawing on the same data and definitions?

Four principles

01

One definition for each number

A figure means the same thing in every view, so teams compare like with like.

02

Start from the team’s question

Each view opens on the question that team asks most often.

03

Choose the chart for the question

Lines for change over time, columns for age, a grid for new themes, lists for cases.

04

Every number leads to its cases

Any figure opens the list of complaints behind it.

The same frame around every view

Moving between views keeps the data and filters in place, so a number found in one view can be checked in another.

Stays the same in every view
  • The top bar and the date of the data
  • Filters: period, channel, product and region
  • The number of complaints in scope
  • Metric definitions, named in the footer
Changes for each team
  • The question at the top of the page
  • The four headline numbers
  • The charts below them
  • The main action: Export audit pack, Share with risk committee, or Export case list

04 · Deliver

A view for each team

Compliance, Risk and Legal each open on their own question, with the same filters, scope and definitions underneath.

Compliance
On-time rate by channel, open cases by age, topics mapped to rules, overdue cases
Risk
Emerging themes by week, volume against severity, complaint rate per 10K accounts
Legal
A review queue, case evidence with personal details hidden, clusters of similar complaints
