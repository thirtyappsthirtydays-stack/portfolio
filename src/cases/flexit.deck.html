01 · Discover

Downloads that never became accounts

Where new users dropped off
Download Open the app Sign-up form Account created First gym visit
Many left before creating an account, so they never reached a gym.

What I found · UX research and a heuristic evaluation

Abrupt sign-upIt felt sudden, with no context
Unclear modelNew users didn’t understand how it worked
No first stepLittle guidance on how to start
Hidden featuresImportant features weren’t introduced
Core insightUnderstanding comes before sign-upNew users needed guidance and reassurance to see the value of FlexIt.

02 · Define

Explain FlexIt, then ask for an account

The redesign had to explain the product to someone who had never used it, and keep account creation quick.

Problem statementFirst-time users were asked to create an account before they understood what FlexIt does or how to use it.
How might weHelp first-time users understand FlexIt and feel confident creating an account?

How I measured success

For usersUnderstandingNew users who understand how paying by the minute works
For the businessSign-upDownloads that become accountsFirst visitNew accounts that make a first gym visit

03 · Develop

Three options, one guided onboarding

I compared each way to reduce drop-off against the cause I had found.

  • Not chosenSimplify the sign-up formNo explanation of the product
  • Not chosenFewer registration stepsNo explanation of the product either
  • ChosenGuided onboardingAddresses the cause: unclear value and functionality

Principles

Four rules for the new onboarding

Show the value firstWhat FlexIt offers and how to use it, before the form
Explain every questionEach personal question says why it’s asked
Point only to what’s newTips for the features a first-time user wouldn’t guess
Keep sign-up shortAsk only for what partner gyms need
What the business needed More accountsTurn downloads into members Less early churnKeep new users from leaving early Faster understandingShow how FlexIt works, quickly

04 · Deliver

Three changes to onboarding

A welcome carousel, information modals and onboarding tooltips, with fewer onboarding screens overall.

Results

More sign-ups, and far fewer people leaving
