Results and reflection

More personal than swiping

In usability testing, people responded well to the guided sign-up and the personal tone. For Olivia, the platform brings sign-ups, profiles and match messages into one system.

What testers said
  • The profile prompts felt more meaningful than dating-app profiles
  • How It Works built trust and made the process clear
  • The date feedback loop felt engaging, and kept people accountable
  • The whole experience felt more personal than swipe-based apps
