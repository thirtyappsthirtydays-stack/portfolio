01 · Discover

Too many funds to research one by one

Investment platforms offer thousands of ETFs and mutual funds. Without good filtering, investors research them one by one, right at the start of building a portfolio.

Competitive reviewThree answers to the same problem
The gap Fidelity More guidanceMore control
  • MorningstarDeepest data, for experts
  • FidelityMany filters, little explanation
  • Charles SchwabEasy start, less control
  • Citi’s ScreenerControl and guidance, together

What I learned

From observing the existing product and 3 rounds of user interviews with 15 investors.

01

Too many options

Thousands of funds, and no quick way to rule most of them out.

02

Unclear criteria

Terms like equity style or target asset mix meant little without an explanation.

03

Hard to compare

Weighing fees against returns across several funds meant a lot of back and forth.

04

No guidance

Newer investors didn’t know where to start, or which filter to pick first.

Core insight

Investors needed a way to narrow the list

Thousands of funds had to come down to the few that fit their goals.

Problem statement

Self-directed investors need to narrow thousands of funds to the few that match their goals, quickly and with confidence, instead of researching funds one by one.

How might we

Give investors control over the whole fund universe, and a clear place to start?

  • ActionScreener visits that end in a buy, auto-invest or saved fund
