Context

Capital ratios, every month

Banks report capital ratios to show how financially strong they are. The numbers have to be right, and on time.

How much capital a bank holds against the risk it carries. The main one is the CET1 ratio. CET1 (Common Equity Tier 1) is a bank’s core capital, mostly its common shares and retained earnings. I calculated the capital side each month.

Problem statement

Analysts spent much of each cycle preparing data and repeating calculations by hand. That added operational risk and left less time for analysis.

How might we

How might we automate repetitive calculation workflows so analysts could focus more on analysis and risk insights?

Approach

From tidier spreadsheets to full automation

I weighed three ways to improve the workflow, from the smallest change to the biggest.

Option 1

Standard spreadsheet templates

Validation rules and version control on the existing spreadsheets.

Not chosenErrors drop gradually. The work stays manual.
Option 2

A central data warehouse

Inputs gathered automatically, calculations still done by analysts.

Not chosenThe calculations stay manual.
Option 3

End-to-end automated calculation

Automated pipelines, the same process every cycle.

ChosenLess manual effort, consistent results, and room to grow as requirements change.

How I measured success

  • Manual effortAnalyst time spent calculating by hand
  • Cycle timeWorking days to finish each reporting cycle
  • AccuracyRestatements after a report goes out
