01 · Discover

Too many funds to research one by one

Competitors MorningstarDeepest data, for experts FidelityMany filters, little explanation Charles SchwabEasy start, less control The gapControl and guidance, together

What I learned from the existing product and 3 rounds of interviews with 15 investors:

Too many optionsNo quick way to rule most funds out
Unclear criteriaTerms like equity style meant little
Hard to compareFees against returns took back and forth
No guidanceNewer investors didn’t know where to start
Core insightInvestors needed a way to narrow the listThousands of funds had to come down to the few that fit their goals.
