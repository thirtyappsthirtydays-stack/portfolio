03 · Develop

Two concepts, tested, then distilled

I tested two clickable concepts before building, then simplified the preferred one into cards.

  • Not takenA · Weekly questionsOne a week, with a place to log the answer
  • PreferredB · A worry-led conversation planIts small-to-deep order carried into the final design
  • FinalConversation cardsThe plan’s order, as decks a family reads aloud together

What I cut to ship: the AI practice partner, recordings and saved answers, for privacy and simplicity.

Usability testing · 3 rounds, 5 participants each

  • Round 130% task completion
  • After round 1One card at a timeInstead of a list, which people skimmed
  • After round 2Progress dots and a clear endSo pairs knew when they were done
  • Round 350% task completion
