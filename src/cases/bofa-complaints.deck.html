What each team asked of the data

ComplianceAre complaints resolved on time and handled within policy?
RiskWhere is customer harm building, and how fast?
LegalWhich complaints carry legal or regulatory exposure?

02 · Define

One source, read three ways

Problem statementCompliance, Risk and Legal relied on complaint data every day. Finding the figures took too long, and the reports weren’t shaped around how each team worked.
How might weGive each team a view built around its own questions, with every view drawing on the same data and definitions?

03 · Develop

A chart for each question

I matched each question to the chart that answers it fastest.

  • ComplianceAre we resolving on time?Lines by channel, against the target
  • ComplianceWhat is overdue?Columns by days open, overdue cases in red
  • RiskWhat is new and growing?A grid of themes by week
  • RiskWhere do volume and severity meet?A scatter of product and issue pairs
  • LegalWhich cases need review?A queue with exposure tags
  • LegalIs this a one-off or a pattern?Bars of similar complaints, with a review threshold

04 · Deliver

A view for each team

Each opens on its own question, with the same filters, scope and definitions underneath.
