Client project · Healthcare · Turkish and English · 2026

Online Consultation

Helping patients and families book a second opinion online

An online booking flow I designed and built for one of Turkey’s most experienced thoracic surgeons. Patients and families go from first visit to a paid, scheduled consultation in four steps, in Turkish or English.

Screens recreated from the live site’s code. Patient details are fictional.
Role
Solo designer and builder: research, service design, flows, UI, bilingual copy, AI-assisted build
Client
My father, Prof. Dr. Semih Halezeroğlu, a thoracic surgeon in private practice. I ran it as a client project, from his brief through his review.
Timeline
Spring 2026, live since, with ongoing updates. It started as app #25 of 30 Apps.
Outcome
A live four-step booking flow with automated WhatsApp confirmations. 35 consultations booked since launch, and the assistant’s messages per booking down from an average of 10 to 2.

What I learned

Insight 1

The person booking is often not the patient

Children book for parents, parents for babies, so every question asks about the patient.

Insight 2

Patients already use WhatsApp

The surgeon prefers WhatsApp video too. Google Meet covers anyone without it.

Insight 3

The review depends on the records

CT, PET-CT and pathology reports must reach him in time to read, and imaging files are large.

Insight 4

The money has to land in a Turkish account

Bank transfer is familiar and free, but nothing confirms it automatically.

Problem statement

Ayten’s family needs a clear, trusted way to book Prof. Halezeroğlu and get her records to him in time, because a surgical decision is waiting on his answer. He needs every booking to arrive paid, scheduled and prepared.

How might we

Make booking Prof. Halezeroğlu so easy that more patients do it, with details, payment and a time handled in one flow, in their own language?

03 · Develop

Three decisions, explored as wireframes

Three choices shaped the flow. Several shipped as working versions first, and the client’s feedback decided which stayed.

Why pay before picking a time

Payment comes before the calendar, so every slot in the surgeon’s week belongs to someone who has already paid.

Decision 1

How should the form ask? For a relative answering about someone else.

Cut after testingA · Long formNine fields on one page. It read like a hospital form.
ShippedB · One question per screenSix questions, with a counter and Enter to continue.
Kept as a side doorC · WhatsApp onlyA pre-filled message, on every screen.
Decision 2

How do records arrive? They need to reach him before the call.

Cut after testingA · Upload in the formCT and PET-CT files are too large for a web form.
Cut after testingB · E-Nabız loginA portal password stored in a sheet.
ShippedC · WhatsApp, after bookingA list of what to send, at least 24 hours ahead.
Decision 3

How does money move? Upfront, into a Turkish account.

Not builtA · Card checkoutFees and set-up before the first booking.
ShippedB · Bank transfer, then confirmFee and IBAN on one screen, then a “sent it?” check.
