FlexIt

Onboarding that explains the app before it asks for anything

FlexIt lets people pay by the minute at partner gyms. Many people downloaded the app and left before creating an account. We redesigned onboarding around what a first-time user needs to understand.

What we found

UX research and a heuristic evaluation of the onboarding flow.

01

Abrupt sign-up

It felt sudden, with no context.

Core insight

Understanding comes before sign-up

New users needed guidance and reassurance to see the value of FlexIt.

02 · Define

Explain FlexIt, then ask for an account

The redesign had to explain the product to someone who had never used it, and keep account creation quick.

Problem statement

First-time users were asked to create an account before they understood what FlexIt does or how to use it.

How might we

How might we help first-time users understand FlexIt and feel confident creating an account?

Three ways to reduce drop-off

We compared each one against the cause we had found.

Option 1

Simplify the sign-up form

A shorter form, with the onboarding flow unchanged.

Not chosenNo explanation of the product.
Option 2

Fewer registration steps

Cut steps from the registration process.

Not chosenNo explanation of the product either.
Option 3

Guided onboarding

Explain the product and support first-time users through it.

ChosenIt addresses the cause: unclear value and functionality.
01

Show the value first

What FlexIt offers and how to use it, before the form.

02

Explain every question

Each personal question says why it’s asked.

03

Point only to what’s new

Tips for the features a first-time user wouldn’t guess.

04

Keep sign-up short

Ask only for what partner gyms need.

A welcome carousel, information modals and onboarding tooltips, with fewer onboarding screens overall.
