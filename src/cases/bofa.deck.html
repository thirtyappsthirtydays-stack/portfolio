Context

Capital ratios, every month

Banks report capital ratios to show how financially strong they are. The numbers have to be right, and on time.

A capital ratio
CapitalMy part Risk-weighted assetsfrom another team
= Capital ratio
How much capital a bank holds against the risk it carries. The main one is the CET1 ratio. CET1 (Common Equity Tier 1) is a bank’s core capital, mostly its common shares and retained earnings.
MonthlyReported inside the bank QuarterlyReported to regulators Every figureChecked before it goes out

I calculated the capital side each month. The users: capital analysts, and the reporting teams who rely on the numbers.

The problem

A calculation held together by spreadsheets and handoffs

Five steps, most of them manual · the pain point in each

  • Gather dataWaiting on handoffs from other teamsInputs from several internal systems and teams
  • Apply rulesThe same calculations, by hand, every monthWhat counts as capital, worked out in spreadsheets
  • ReconcileMismatches found and fixed by handThe sources matched against each other
  • ValidateRoom for error, and operational riskEvery result checked before it goes out
  • ReportLittle time left for analysisMonthly inside the bank, quarterly to regulators
Problem statementAnalysts spent much of each cycle preparing data and repeating calculations by hand. That added operational risk and left less time for analysis.

Options

From tidier spreadsheets to full automation

How might weAutomate repetitive calculation workflows so analysts could focus more on analysis and risk insights?

I weighed three ways to improve the workflow, from the smallest change to the biggest.

  • Not chosenStandard spreadsheet templatesErrors drop gradually. The work stays manual.
  • Not chosenA central data warehouseInputs gathered automatically. The calculations stay manual.
  • ChosenEnd-to-end automated calculationLess manual effort, consistent results, and room to grow as requirements change

How I measured success

For analystsManual effortAnalyst time spent calculating by hand
For the businessCycle time: working days per cycleAccuracy: restatements after a report goes out

Solution

The rules, written into a reporting tool

With our technology partners, I defined the logic and data each calculation needed, then built it into an automated reporting tool in MicroStrategy.

Calculation logicThe same steps for computing the ratios, every cycle
Data integrationThe data sources the calculations need, linked to the tool
Workflow automationThe repeated manual steps run automatically
Validation checksResults tested against the official definitions before reporting
